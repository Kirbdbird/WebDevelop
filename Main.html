Some Information about Fried Chicken

  • Fried Chicken is a dish consisting of chicken pieces that have been battered or breaded and then deep fried.
  • Fried chicken is a popular dish in many countries, including the United States, South Korea, and Japan.
  • Fried chicken is often served with sides such as mashed potatoes, coleslaw, and biscuits.
  • Fried chicken can be made with different types of chicken, including wings, drumsticks, and breasts.
  • Fried chicken can be seasoned with a variety of spices and herbs to create different flavors.
